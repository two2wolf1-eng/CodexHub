# M61.10-M61.14 Business Permission Debug

## GSD

- Goal: model Business quota visibility by role without reading UI state.
- Scope: role matrix, current-account check plan, seat type matrix, workspace credit matrix, and permission checkpoints.
- Non-scope: no automatic role discovery by page scraping, no workspace admin mutation.
- Acceptance: permission probe supports owner, admin, analytics viewer, member, and unknown.
- Hard boundaries: unknown role requires a human checkpoint.
- Risk: high.

## Rounds Covered

11. M61.10 Business Role Matrix: owner/admin can represent workspace-level readiness; member is own-quota only.
12. M61.11 Current Account Role Check Plan: role must come from App Server metadata or human declaration.
13. M61.12 Seat Type Matrix: seat readiness is stored as booleans and counts only.
14. M61.13 Workspace Credit Matrix: workspace credits and spend controls are count/status concepts.
15. M61.14 Permission Failure Checkpoints: insufficient role, workspace mismatch, and analytics unavailable map to human checkpoints.

## Evidence

- `BusinessQuotaPermissionProbeSchema` rejects member overreach.
- Unknown or blocked permission records require a human checkpoint kind.
- Supervisor rehearsal accepts a role declaration but does not treat it as live authority.
