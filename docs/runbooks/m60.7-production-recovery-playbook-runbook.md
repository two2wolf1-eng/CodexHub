# M60.7 Production Recovery Playbook

Purpose:

- Give operators a production-safe mapping from M58 diagnosis states to recovery
  actions during M60 readiness and canary work.

## Diagnosis Map

`completed`:

- Do not recover. Move to verification, review, and audit export.

`failed_quota`:

- Allowed: wait, refresh quota snapshot, select another ready account through
  scheduler metadata.
- Requires approval: switch account.
- Stop: do not bypass quota or workspace limits.

`failed_auth`:

- Allowed: create HumanCheckpoint for login recovery.
- Requires approval: login_recover, switch_account.
- Stop: do not read credentials, MFA, cookies, storage, or session material.

`waiting_approval`:

- Allowed: wait, resend summary, show approval request metadata.
- Requires approval: any action that would execute, mutate files, interrupt a
  turn, or change account/client selection.
- Stop: do not infer approval from request body content.

`tool_stuck`:

- Allowed: wait, diagnose event heartbeat, mark blocked.
- Requires approval: interrupt_turn, clean_background_terminals, resume, fork,
  transfer.
- Stop: do not kill terminals or processes silently.

`model_stalled`:

- Allowed: wait, summarize stalled event metadata, create recovery dry-run.
- Requires approval: interrupt_turn, resume, fork, transfer.
- Stop: do not create a new live turn without approval.

`desktop_ui_frozen`:

- Allowed: read-only Electron/CDP metadata, OS process metadata summary.
- Requires approval: restart_desktop.
- Stop: do not use Runtime.evaluate, DOM mutation, click, type, or main
  inspector actions.

`app_server_unresponsive`:

- Allowed: metadata-only App Server health check and drift gate.
- Requires approval: reconnect_app_server, restart_desktop if needed.
- Stop: do not silently reconnect with new authority.

## Recovery Action Policy

- `wait`: low risk, no approval if it only updates status metadata.
- `login_recover`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, HumanCheckpoint required.
- `switch_account`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, scheduler lease required.
- `reconnect_app_server`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, protocol drift must
  be compatible.
- `restart_desktop`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, OS process observer must
  be metadata-only.
- `interrupt_turn`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, task diagnosis required.
- `clean_background_terminals`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, no broad kill.
- `resume`, `fork`, `transfer`: high risk, dry-run plus approval, isolated
  worktree and task leases required.

## Evidence Requirements

- Store diagnosis id, recovery run id, status, blocker count, summary hash,
  evidence refs, and audit ids.
- Do not store raw prompt, raw diff, raw path, raw body, token, cookie, session,
  MFA, storage, credentials, or profile path.
- Every high-risk recovery must leave a dry-run record and approval trace before
  any live effect is considered.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if:

- A recovery skips dry-run or approval.
- A route calls an adapter directly.
- A recovery reads or stores secret/session material.
- A recovery mutates desktop UI through click/type/DOM/evaluate.
- A scheduler lease is missing for account, client, profile, thread, task, quota,
  or worktree.
