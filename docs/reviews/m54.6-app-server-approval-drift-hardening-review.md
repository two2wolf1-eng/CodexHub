## Boundary Review

The M54.6 implementation preserves these invariants:

- Approval bridge records never allow silent approval.
- Approval bridge records do not store raw proposals or approval secrets.
- Approval request ids, thread ids, turn ids, item ids, proposals, and decision
  labels are hashed before output.
- Protocol drift reports store baseline/observed schema hashes and counts only.
- Unknown or incompatible protocol drift blocks live dispatch.
- The adapter remains a capability provider and does not grant authority.

## Residual Risks

- Approval bridge records are not yet persisted by Supervisor/orchestrator store
  wiring.
- Live App Server dispatch remains future work and must stay behind policy,
  approval, evidence, and audit gates.
