# M75.5 Real Rehearsal Acceptance Review

## Findings

No open security findings in the M75.5 envelope design. The acceptance route records metadata-only state and refuses caller-supplied raw selectors, endpoints, scripts, prompts, DOM, request/response bodies, or credential material.

## Boundary Review

- Live acceptance does not create a new adapter boundary.
- M75 child operations remain governed by their own surfaces, manifests, approvals, and authority.
- The acceptance result cannot claim a live touch unless `realBoundaryReached` and `liveClientTouched` are recorded by the server-side path.

## Residual Risk

The acceptance record depends on upstream M75/M76 operation truth. If a child record is incomplete, the run should remain `readiness_blocked` or `failed_requires_manual_repair`, not `real_live_accepted`.
